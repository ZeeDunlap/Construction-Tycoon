A tycoon game developed in Unity were the player must successfully start a construction business

Developed by Zee Dunlap, Andrew Foster, Colin Kinzel, and Van Thaun Tran 

Construction Tycoon is a game about successfully building a construction company from the ground up. 
Starting out, the player will have little money, basic tools, and will only be able to accept small jobs. 
Completing jobs will grant the player money which can be used to buy better equipment or hire employees. 
Jobs will also provide experience which will grant access to more complex jobs.  

To finish a job, the player will have to complete certain actions that are related to tasks contained in the job. 
Jobs will start out small, like replacing a light fixture or installing a door. 
Once the player hires employees, the employees can be sent to complete small jobs without help from the player. 
More employees also mean that the player can complete larger jobs, eventually doing new construction of entire buildings. 
Better tools will also help the player complete bigger jobs and complete jobs more quickly. 

To win, the player must create a successful company by navigating the challenges of being an entrepreneur. 
Although the game does have a final goal, after which the player has won, 
they can continue their company for as long as they like afterwards. 
The player loses if they go bankrupt.
